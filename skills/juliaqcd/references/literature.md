# Papers to read for scales and improvement coefficients

When the user needs a physical scale, a lattice spacing, or a c_SW and has
no paper of their own, propose papers from this list. Read the paper and
quote the value with page, table, or equation number; never quote a value
from this list or from memory (entry skill, "Values from the literature").
Check that the paper's action, Nf, and β range match the user's ensemble:
a value for the Wilson gauge action does not apply to the Iwasaki action,
and a c_SW for thin links does not apply to stout-smeared clover fermions.
Identifiers were checked on arXiv on 2026-11-11.

## Definitions of the scales

| Scale | Paper |
|---|---|
| r0, from the static force: r² F(r) at r = r0 | R. Sommer, Nucl. Phys. B411 (1994) 839, arXiv:hep-lat/9310022 |
| t0, from the gradient flow: t² ⟨E(t)⟩ = 0.3 | M. Lüscher, JHEP 08 (2010) 071, arXiv:1006.4518 |
| w0, from the gradient flow: t d/dt (t² ⟨E⟩) = 0.3 at t = w0² | S. Borsanyi et al. (BMW), JHEP 09 (2012) 010, arXiv:1203.4469 |
| Review of scale setting | R. Sommer, PoS LATTICE2013 (2014) 015, arXiv:1401.3270 |

Pure gauge theory has no experimental input: a value of r0 or √t0 in fm
for Nf = 0 is a convention. Ask the user which one they follow.

## Improvement coefficient c_SW (thin links, Wilson gauge action)

| What | Paper |
|---|---|
| Nf = 0, non-perturbative c_SW | M. Lüscher, S. Sint, R. Sommer, P. Weisz, U. Wolff, Nucl. Phys. B491 (1997) 323, arXiv:hep-lat/9609035 |
| Nf = 2, non-perturbative c_SW | K. Jansen and R. Sommer, Nucl. Phys. B530 (1998) 185 (erratum B643 (2002) 517), arXiv:hep-lat/9803017 |

These papers give c_SW as a function of β for their gauge action and Nf.
With stout-smeared links or another gauge action c_SW is different; ask
for the paper that matches.
